# V2.4 — Smart Schedule Recommendation

## Goal
Merekomendasikan task yang paling cocok untuk setiap free slot.

Gunakan:
- Priority Engine
- Deadline Risk Engine
- Free Time Detection

## Ranking Factors
1. priority
2. deadline
3. remaining duration
4. slot duration
5. task constraints
6. existing schedule

## Example
FREE SLOT: 15:00–17:00

Database:
Critical / 90 min / deadline tomorrow

Essay:
Medium / 60 min / deadline 7 days

Recommendation:
15:00–16:30 — Database Assignment

## UX
Gunakan label "Recommended", bukan "Automatically scheduled".

Actions:
- Accept
- Dismiss
- Reschedule

Recommendation harus deterministic, testable, dan tidak duplicate.
